The Vision

A world waiting to be explored.

GENESIS is at the concept stage. The goal is to develop an original game world where exploration, discovery and meaningful progression shape the player's journey. The ideas below represent the intended direction, not confirmed or completed gameplay features.

VISION / 01

Open-World Exploration

An expansive world concept filled with locations, discoveries and opportunities for adventure.

VISION / 02

Anime-Inspired Combat

Aiming for dynamic battles, unique abilities and memorable encounters.

VISION / 03

Character Progression

A vision for character growth, developing skills and discovering new possibilities.

VISION / 04

A Living World

Exploring ideas for an immersive world with environments and experiences that reward curiosity.
